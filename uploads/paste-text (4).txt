Create a premium multi-step Contract Creation flow for Depoit.

This is the MOST IMPORTANT flow in the product.

The experience should feel like:

* opening a premium financial account,
* calm,
* trustworthy,
* guided,
* emotionally reassuring,
* simple and elegant.

Avoid:

* enterprise forms,
* ERP-style UI,
* long boring forms,
* generic CRUD layouts.

# Flow Structure

Step 1 — Property

* Property address
* Commune
* Region
* Property type

Step 2 — Participants

* Landlord
* Tenant
* Optional broker
* Invitation by email

Step 3 — Financial Terms

* Monthly rent
* Deposit amount
* Contract dates
* CLP formatting

Step 4 — Digital Signature

* Simulated signature experience
* Trust-oriented language
* Legal/security messaging

Step 5 — Deposit Custody

* Payment simulation
* Deposit received state
* Secure custody confirmation
* Success experience

# UX Requirements

Use:

* premium stepper,
* smooth transitions,
* progress indicators,
* large spacing,
* calm typography,
* clear hierarchy,
* modern cards,
* subtle animations,
* onboarding-focused language.

# Important

Each step should:

* feel lightweight,
* reduce anxiety,
* guide the user clearly,
* explain why information is needed.

# Trust Layer

Throughout the wizard include:

* security messaging,
* custody protection messaging,
* transparency messaging,
* traceability reassurance.

# Final Success Screen

The completion state should feel emotionally rewarding and trustworthy.

Include:

* contract created confirmation,
* custody activated,
* next steps,
* timeline preview,
* participant invitations sent.

The overall experience should feel comparable to:

* Stripe onboarding,
* Mercury account opening,
* Airbnb host onboarding.

Prioritize:

* emotional trust,
* clarity,
* elegance,
* simplicity,
* perceived professionalism.
