## Design

**Design References**: Stripe, Mercury, Linear, Ramp, Notion

- Primary: #0F172A (dark navy), #1E293B (slate)
- Accent: #10B981 (trust-green), #059669 (green-dark)
- Background: #FFFFFF, #F8FAFC (light gray)
